# Research Record

**Protocol:** `research-record@0.1.0`  
**Mode:** agent-guided preservation and integration  
**Invocation:** “Record this to the repository.”

## Purpose and applicability

Preserve a substantive contribution so another worker can find its evidence, understand its scope, and continue. Use at meaningful closure after a result, decision, correction, disagreement, failed direction, or useful imaginative opening.

A substantive unit is not every message or tool call. Do not manufacture a change to justify a record.

## Inputs

Use the current objective, exact relevant source revision, existing authoritative record locations, available evidence, and the visible material actually supplied. Identify the permitted publication audience before preservation.

Preserving public project work does not authorize copying unrelated private records, protected source material, credentials, hidden reasoning, or platform instructions. Record omitted and unavailable coverage.

## Procedure

1. **Recover the existing homes.** Inspect only the relevant instructions, work and decision records, and evidence needed for this contribution. Reuse stable entity IDs. Do not create another live dashboard.
2. **Distill without flattening.** Separate source attribution, exact derivation under assumptions, observed result, interpretation, decision, hypothesis, and proposal. An imaginative seed needs an origin and a question; promotion to an experiment adds an observable, inputs, and bounds when they exist.
3. **Route by contribution.** Keep exact source and raw results with their provenance. Place concise interpretation in a linked reading layer. Record changed acceptance or ownership in its authoritative home. Preserve failed directions and conditions that might reopen them.
4. **Preserve dialogue accurately.** Store only the visible messages actually available and authorized for this audience. Keep exact supplied text where requested, with explicit coverage. Distinguish an actual model response from an attributed summary, a prepared request, an unsuccessful call, or an imagined perspective.
5. **Connect and supersede.** Link the affected claim, decision, source, run, or work item. Preserve original observations and old decisions; append a correction or successor instead of rewriting their historical meaning.
6. **Check the relevant risk.** Validate changed references and content identities; inspect the bounded diff and concurrent changes. Use scientific or executable checks only where needed for the claim. A structural record validator cannot adjudicate the science.
7. **Publish and verify within scope.** Use the actual granted publication audience and an expected-head or equivalent non-destructive integration mechanism. Verify persisted content and report the exact result. An uncertain publication outcome must be reconciled before retry.
8. **Continue.** Select the next useful action under current authority. Recording does not end an ongoing research objective.

## Model provenance and replay

Record the provider and requested model, the reported model/version when exposed, the exact supplied visible input identity, relevant exposed options, actual completion state, returned output identity, and substantive review or corrections. Unknown values remain unknown. Different role names do not establish independent evidence.

Separate three actions:

- **Record replay:** deterministic validation, retrieval, or rendering of existing records.
- **Experiment replay:** another execution under a versioned run specification and appropriate authority.
- **Model re-query:** a new inference request whose output may differ, even if the visible input is unchanged.

Do not treat cached output as a new observation. Do not retry an inference or external effect of uncertain outcome blindly. A corrected successor receives a new attempt identity and a link to the earlier attempt.

## Output

Emit a receipt with the actual contribution, input and output references, evidence level and scope, publication effects, coverage limits, changed decision or useful seed, and next action. One authoritative home per fact permits several derived views and explicitly competing interpretations.
